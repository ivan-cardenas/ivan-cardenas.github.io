CrossTwin

A digital twin of how a city's systems connect.

See how a change in one urban sector moves through the whole city.

01 · Geospatial foundation

One city, from province to neighbourhood

Land cover, terrain, buildings and boundaries sit together in PostGIS, nested from province down to neighbourhood. The layers import from Dutch open data such as PDOK, CBS and the Klimaateffectatlas, and from OpenStreetMap.

Province › City › District › Neighbourhood

02 · Causal network

Changes travel along a causal graph

The graph follows DPSIR: Driver, Pressure, State, Impact, Response. Stored models recalculate from their inputs, so a change travels on wherever the edges are built; just under half of the graph's 105 edges are computed so far, and the rest are partial or documented gaps.

03 · Water supply

From well to tap, and what leaks

Extraction, treatment and pipe networks feed production, coverage and operating cost, and real losses are traced to the pipes where leakage is presumed as non-revenue water. Population drives demand on through to supply security and service time.

04 · Urban heat

Where the city holds its heat

Land surface temperature comes from Landsat thermal bands, and sky view factor from the surface model. For a chosen view and hour, SOLWEIG computes mean radiant temperature, UTCI and PET, with buildings and trees casting the shade.

LST · SVF · Tmrt · UTCI · PET

05 · Housing and population

More people, more homes needed

CBS forecasts give the population, and population divided by household size gives the homes needed. The dashboard sets that against supply, affordability stress and pipeline projects, and a what-if slider tests other futures without saving them.

06 · Built-up fabric and nature

The fabric the other systems stand on

Building stock and type mix, street density, facilities and green space are summarised for any administrative unit. Soil maps and land cover add the infiltration of a rain event.

07 · What-if editing

Draw a building, then undo it

Staff can draw a building on the map: the land cover around it changes, the surface model is patched, and heat results it touches are flagged as stale until they are rerun. Restore the backup to undo it; sliders test scenarios without writing to the baseline.

  • New_Units → LandCover
  • Buildings → DSM
  • SVF, Tmrt, UTCI marked stale
