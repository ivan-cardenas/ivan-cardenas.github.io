## Summary

I am a Geospatial (GIS) Specialist with 8 years of experience in spatial data pipelines, Urban Digital Twin platforms, and production-grade Web-GIS applications for urban planning and environmental impact assessment. My experience spans Territorial Development, Cartography for Land Use Planning, Geodatabase Structuring, Hydrological Modeling, Spatial Analysis, Remote Sensing, Environmental Impact Assessment, and Research. Currently I am a PhD Candidate in the Urban Planning and Management Department at the University of Twente with a research project focused on Digital Twins as an enhanced 3D Planning Support System.
