### Subject: Application for Solutions Architect – Professional Services

Dear Planet Hiring Team, 
<br><br>
Urban systems are changing faster than the tools we use to understand them, and satellite-derived Earth observation data is one of the few technologies capable of closing that gap at scale. This is precisely why I am applying for the Solutions Architect role on Planet's Professional Services team. My background combines eight years of applied geospatial work, a PhD in Urban Digital Twins currently in its third year at the University of Twente, and hands-on experience designing and delivering spatial data platforms for real-world planning decisions, a combination I believe maps directly onto what your team needs.
<br><br>
In my current research, we develop CrossTwin, an open-source Urban Digital Twin platform built on GeoDjango, PostGIS, React, and TiTiler, designed to replace manual SQL-driven workflows with a DAG-based spatial query architecture. Across three urban sectors (drinking water supply, heat stress, and housing provision) we translate complex, multi-source geospatial datasets into structured, decision-ready outputs for planners and policymakers. Alongside this, I have built and deployed interactive decision support tools for real operational use: a Streamlit web application for heat stress scenario analysis (https://is2h4c-dt.streamlit.app/) and a Python Panel dashboard hosted on Hugging Face Spaces for Vitens, the Dutch drinking water authority, enabling scenario-based modelling of supply security, environmental impacts, and costs across multiple future planning horizons (https://huggingface.co/spaces/cygnus26/Vitalens). These tools were designed from the ground up to be used by planners and engineers who are not software developers, which required careful attention to user experience, workflow design, and communicating uncertainty clearly. This work has taught me that the most technically elegant solution fails if it does not speak to the stakeholder's actual question. Listening carefully, then translating requirements into a coherent architecture, is a skill I have practiced consistently with both institutional clients and academic collaborators.
<br><br>
Beyond research, I have delivered applied geospatial solutions in consulting roles across Colombia and the Netherlands, including hydrological modelling (HEC-HMS, HEC-RAS), UAV imagery pipelines, environmental impact assessment, and Web-GIS development using Mapbox GL and JavaScript. At Philips, I replaced a manual biodiversity spreadsheet workflow with an interactive global manufacturing site map integrating satellite and API-based land-use data, a project that mirrors the kind of customer-facing solution design Planet's Professional Services team produces daily.
<br><br>
I am proficient in Python for geospatial applications (GeoPandas, rasterio, GDAL), experienced with QGIS and ArcGIS, and comfortable working in Agile cross-functional teams. I regularly use AI tools including Claude and LLM-assisted coding to accelerate prototyping and demo development. I have designed and delivered university courses on Digital Twins and Urban Heat Modelling, and presented at ISPRS international conferences, which reflects both my technical depth and my ability to communicate complex methods to diverse audiences.
<br><br>

Planet's mission of making global change actionable resonates directly with the core argument of my research: that geospatial data only generates societal value when it is connected to a decision. I would welcome the opportunity to bring this perspective to your customers, your pre-sales process, and your Professional Services team in Haarlem.
<br><br>

Thank you for your time and consideration. I look forward to the possibility of contributing to Planet's work.
